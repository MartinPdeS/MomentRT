Choosing a mesh for detector collection#

simulation.convergence_study(...) compares detector power and footprint sampling across explicit finite meshes. It keeps the sample, source, detector apertures, wavelength, and boundaries fixed, returning a DetectorConvergenceResult without changing the original configuration. See the worked refinement example for comparison plots and a fluence volume with instrument overlays.

Running a study#

Configure a finite Diffusion, SP3, or P3 simulation with at least one named detector, then supply meshes from coarse to fine:

study = simulation.convergence_study(
    mesh_shapes=[
        (9, 7, 7),
        (13, 10, 10),
        (17, 13, 13),
        (25, 19, 19),
    ],
    power_rtol=0.01,
    area_rtol=0.02,
    power_atol=0 * ureg.watt,
    required_consecutive=2,
)

figure = study.plot(
    power_unit="mW",
    area_unit="mm^2",
    show=False,
)

print(f"Recommended mesh shape: {study.recommended_shape}")

Every shape contains three node counts of at least two. Each successive mesh must refine at least one axis and coarsen none. At least two levels are required, but two consecutive passing refinements need at least three resolved levels. The study evaluates all supplied levels rather than stopping at the first apparent plateau.

These tolerances are numerical choices, not claims about transport-model error. A study uses the configured source wavelength. Repeat it at demanding wavelengths when assessing a spectral experiment.

Power and area criteria#

Let \(P_{d,k}\) be detector power at mesh level \(k\) and \(A_{d,k}\) the sampled detector area. Its geometric disk area is \(A_d=\pi r_d^2\). The source has sampled area \(A_{s,k}\) and geometric area \(A_s\): a disk area for SurfaceSource or the complete face area for UniformSurfaceSource.

\[e_{d,k}=\left|\frac{A_{d,k}}{A_d}-1\right|, \qquad e_{s,k}=\left|\frac{A_{s,k}}{A_s}-1\right|.\]

Both errors compare sampled areas with geometry, rather than only with the previous mesh. Two coarse meshes could sample the same incorrect area. Source normalization preserves incoming power even when a footprint is poorly sampled, so energy conservation cannot replace this area check.

Successive powers pass when

\[|P_{d,k}-P_{d,k-1}| \le P_{\mathrm{atol}}+r_P|P_{d,k}|.\]

The report also records relative change \(|P_{d,k}-P_{d,k-1}|/|P_{d,k}|\). The first value is NaN because no previous estimate exists. Two zero powers have relative change zero. A change to zero from nonzero power has infinite relative change, but may still pass an explicitly supplied absolute tolerance. Missing observations never pass.

Defaults are power_rtol=0.01, area_rtol=0.02, zero absolute power tolerance, and required_consecutive=2. A nonzero power_atol requires an explicit power quantity; choose it according to the numerical accuracy needed for a small signal.

Recommendation rule#

A level passes only when every detector passes its successive power check, all source and detector area errors are within area_rtol, and there are no unresolved footprints or reported negative fields at that level.

The finest end of the sequence must have at least required_consecutive uninterrupted passing refinements. A later failure discards an earlier apparent plateau. Among candidates in that final stable sequence, the first acceptable mesh must also agree with the final level’s detector powers:

\[|P_{d,k}-P_{d,K}| \le P_{\mathrm{atol}}+r_P|P_{d,K}|.\]

This prevents small successive changes from accumulating into a large discrepancy with the finest supplied observation. The recommendation is evidence from a finite sequence, not a rigorous error bound relative to an infinitely refined solution.

recommended_mesh and recommended_shape are None and converged is False when the criteria are not met. The final mesh is not automatically declared sufficient.

Inspecting the report#

The report retains:

  • meshes, mesh_shapes, node_counts, and tetrahedron_counts.

  • Unit-bearing detector_power[name], detector_sampled_area[name], and detector_geometric_area[name].

  • source_sampled_area and source_geometric_area.

  • power_relative_change[name] and power_relative_to_finest[name].

  • detector_area_relative_error[name] and source_area_relative_error.

  • meets_tolerances, stable_refinements, and level_errors.

  • results, containing the available finite solution at each level.

records groups quantities and diagnostics by mesh level:

for record in study.records:
    measurement = record["detectors"]["reflection"]

    print(f"Mesh: {record['mesh_shape']}")

    print(f"Collected power: {measurement['collected_power'].to('mW'):~P}")

    print(f"Aperture area error: {measurement['area_relative_error']:.2%}")

The plot compares powers and sampled footprint areas against mesh node count; dashed area curves are the geometric references. Missing observations remain gaps.

Unresolved levels and configuration errors#

If no quadrature point samples a source disk, the level records SourceResolutionError, source area zero, and results[k]=None. Detector powers and areas for that unsolved level are unknown and NaN. The study continues on finer meshes.

If a solve succeeds but an aperture contains no quadrature points, DetectorResolutionError is recorded for that detector. Its sampled area is zero and its power is unknown. Other measurements remain available. An unresolved level breaks a stable sequence. Negative nodal fluence or escaping irradiance in an aperture is preserved and flagged rather than clipped or accepted for a recommendation.

Both resolution exceptions inherit from ValueError, preserving existing exception handling for single solves. Other errors, such as an aperture outside the sample or unsupported index mismatch, propagate as configuration errors rather than being disguised as coarse-mesh failures.

This study assesses resolution of the tested observables and footprints. It does not establish P1, SP3, or P3 accuracy against RTE, validate material spectra or detector optics, or guarantee convergence of unrelated local field values. See Finite-sample P1 solver and Finite-aperture surface detectors for those distinctions.
